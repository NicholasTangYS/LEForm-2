We may collect the following personal information:

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To provide, maintain, and improve our Tax file conversion services
  • Account Management: To create and manage your user account
  • Payment Processing: To process transactions and manage your token balance
  • Communication: To send service updates, support responses, and important notices
  • Security: To detect, prevent, and address technical issues and fraudulent activities
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws and regulations
  • Service Improvement: To analyze usage patterns and improve our AI algorithms

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the PDPA 2010, we process your personal data based on:

  • Consent: You have given consent for specific purposes
  • Contract: Processing is necessary to fulfill our service agreement with you
  • Legal Obligation: Processing is required by Malaysian law
  • Legitimate Interest: Processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests

We may share your personal data with:

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to:

8. Your Rights Under PDPA

Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request access to your personal data
